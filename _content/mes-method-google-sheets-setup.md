The MES Method workbook has 84 sessions laid out across 12 weeks. I built it around mobility, engine, and strength, with about an hour of training each day. Here’s what to set before logging your first workout.

1. **Make a copy.** Open the workbook link and make your own copy in Google Drive. Read the **Start Here** tab for the program overview.
2. **Choose your setup.** On the **Setup** tab, start with Block 1 if this is your first time. Pick a Zone 2 activity you’ll stick with and choose a focus area. The focus area determines which finisher exercises appear. Use the exercise overrides for any movements you want to swap.
3. **Log your next session.** On the **Log** tab, select **Go to Next Session**. For Zone 2, enter your time, distance, and average heart rate. On lift days, log the reps and weight for each set. Add weight next time once you hit the target reps across all sets.
4. **Check your progress.** After a few sessions, open the **Dashboard** to see your consistency and training trends. Weeks 4, 8, and 12 reduce the number of sets for recovery. When you finish the block, make a fresh copy for the next one and keep the old workbook as your record.
