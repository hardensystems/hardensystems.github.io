If you wear retainers, you already know the drill: soak them, brush them, hope for the best. But soaking alone doesn't really cut it, and brushing only gets you so far — bacteria and buildup happen anyway, and most of the "quick fix" cleaning tablets on the market rely on harsh chemicals you're putting right back in your mouth every single day.

That's what pushed me to try the **Clelifove Ultrasonic Retainer Cleaner Machine** — a 48kHz ultrasonic cleaning pod with a 200ML capacity that also works for dentures, aligners, mouth guards, night guards, dental trays, braces, and even toothbrushes and jewelry. ## The tank material is what sold me

Most cleaning tools you drop in water are made from some kind of plastic, and plastic plus heat plus daily use is exactly the combination that raises red flags for a lot of people trying to avoid unnecessary chemical exposure.

This cleaner uses a tank made of **SUS304 stainless steel** — a food-grade steel that's the same standard used in kitchen equipment and medical-adjacent tools. That single detail matters more than it sounds like it should, because it means you're not relying on a mystery plastic to hold whatever solution you're using, day after day, right next to something that goes in your mouth.

## A cleaning solution you can actually trust

Because the tank is food-grade stainless steel, I didn't have to use a proprietary cleaning tablet packed with fragrance, dye, and chemicals I can't pronounce. I mixed my own solution — half distilled white vinegar, half water — and used that instead.

Vinegar is a mild, natural acid that's been used for cleaning for centuries, and pairing it with a food-safe tank means there's no concern about the solution reacting with or breaking down the material over time. If you've already swapped out seed oils, ditched the chemical-heavy cleaning sprays under your sink, and started reading ingredient labels on everything, this is one more easy swap in that same direction.

## Simple to use, no guesswork

The process is about as low-effort as it gets: drop the retainer in, press start, done. It runs through an ultrasonic cleaning cycle, and it also includes UV sanitization as an added layer of disinfection — no additional chemicals required for that part either.

It didn't produce a flawless, streak-free result every time — there was occasionally a light cloudy residue left behind after a cycle. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing going in. Compared to soaking alone or brushing alone, the difference was noticeable. It got noticeably cleaner, and I never had to touch a chemical tablet to get there.

For the price, this is a genuinely solid upgrade for anyone who wears retainers or other oral appliances and wants a cleaning method that doesn't rely on synthetic chemical tablets.
