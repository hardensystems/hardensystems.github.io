I wanted to finish my MES Method training spreadsheet and give it a home online. Earl Nightingale’s *The Strangest Secret* gave me a way to keep that one goal in view for 30 days.

My mom handed me the book before we boarded a flight for vacation. Nightingale compares your mind to a garden that grows whatever you plant in it. His warning about following people who are themselves following someone else stuck with me. I read the book, listened to the audiobook several times, and decided to try the exercise.

## What I put on the card

Nightingale suggests writing a single goal on an index card and keeping it in mind for 30 days. I wrote that by August 4 I would finish the MES Method spreadsheet and put it online as the first product on my links page.

I finished early. Along the way, the links page turned into my own website, with a shop where I could put the program. The exercise made me pay closer attention to what I focus on and how I talk to myself. I’m looking forward to choosing the next goal.
