A slow leak from our reverse-osmosis system ruined board games and shelving before I spotted it. After [fixing the RO system](/lucas-harden/content/apec-ro-90-leaking-fix/), I wanted a warning on my phone the next time water started collecting.

I mounted the RO system lower, put a drain tray underneath it, and placed a YoLink sensor at the tray’s lowest point. To set it up, I plugged in the hub and paired the sensor by scanning its QR code in the YoLink app. I started with four sensors, adding the other three near the water heater, washing machine, and main water line in the utility closet.

I picked YoLink for its long-range connection and replaceable AAA batteries. The placement mattered most in my setup. Put the sensor where water is likely to collect first, so it can alert you while the leak is still small.
