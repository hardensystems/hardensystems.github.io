I came downstairs one morning to find my APEC RO-90 reverse osmosis system had leaked into my basement overnight. Lost some shelving, a few board games, and had a genuine mess to clean up — all sitting right underneath the unit. Here's how I actually tracked down the problem, what didn't fix it, and what finally did.

## Diagnosing the leak

The system has three filter stages down low and two more up top, and the drip was coming from up near the top — somewhere around the tubing or the housing itself. I pulled the whole unit down to get a clear look, and found a small drip coming from a threaded port. It looked like the fitting wasn't fully seated.

## What I tried first (that didn't fix it)

My first theory was a simple one: a John Guest-style threaded fitting that looked slightly unscrewed. While I had things apart, I went ahead and changed all five filters, replaced the O-rings with fresh food-grade grease, and wrapped the suspect threaded fitting in PTFE tape before reassembling everything.

I let the tank fill and ran it for a few hours, confident that was the fix.

It wasn't. Still leaking from the same spot.

## What actually fixed it

After a few back-and-forth messages with APEC's support team, I ordered the **filter housing cap assembly** — a pre-built replacement for the entire top housing, fittings included. Once I swapped that whole assembly in, the leak stopped completely. Turns out the issue wasn't a single loose fitting — it was the housing cap itself.

## Redesigning the setup to prevent it happening again

Fixing the leak wasn't enough for me — I wanted to make sure that *if* it ever happens again, it's a minor cleanup instead of a repeat disaster. So I made a few changes:

- **Remounted the whole system lower**, closer to the basement floor instead of up near the ceiling
- **Added a boot tray underneath**, angled toward the floor drain
- **Drilled a small drain hole** near the edge of the tray so any future leak has somewhere to go
- **Installed a YoLink water sensor** inside the tray to catch a leak early next time
- **Cleaned up and clamped the tubing** so nothing's loose or under tension
