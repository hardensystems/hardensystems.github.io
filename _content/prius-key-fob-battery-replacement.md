If your 2010–2015 Prius key fob needs a new battery, you can open the shell with the mechanical key tucked inside it. For the fob shown in the video, you’ll need a CR1632 battery and a small flathead screwdriver.

1. Press the release marked **PUSH** and pull out the mechanical key.
2. Insert the tip of the key into the notch at the bottom of the fob. Twist it clockwise to separate the shell.
3. Lift out the circuit board carefully. The battery is on the underside. Use the small flathead to ease it out.
4. Fit the new battery in the same orientation. In the fob shown, the beveled face points toward the circuit board.
5. Put the board back with the three white switches aligned with the lock, unlock, and alarm buttons. Snap the shell shut, slide the key back in, and test the buttons.
